# Skill: titles

The title and the opening line work as a pair. When they say the same thing, half the space that
earns attention is wasted.

- Same promise, different words: the title shares no content words with the hook line.
- Subject in the first three words. Not "This is how the...", not "Watch what happens when...".
- Concrete beats adjective: a number, a name or a place beats "insane", "ultimate", "crazy".
- At most two words in capitals; more reads as spam.
- At most 60 characters; the first 40 must carry the subject, since mobile cuts there.
- About the payoff, not the setup, and never a quote of the transcript.
- Never invent a figure or a result to make it concrete. With no number in the clip, write it without one.
- Write in the speaker's language mix (see the language rule).
