# Skill: graphic beats

Learnt from 84 Before/After editor portfolio reels. The edit that wins is not "captions plus music":
whenever the speaker names something, it appears on screen on that word, about one graphic every
two to three seconds, and the speaker is on screen only part of the time.

What to show for what is said:
- A number that is the point (money, a count, a percentage, a year, a duration): `count_up`, the figure exactly as said, with a 1-4 word label from the speech. Put it `behind` the speaker when it is the line's main idea.
- Three or more items said in a row (reasons, steps, symptoms, tools): `list`, each item appearing on its own word. Negations ("not X, not Y, not Z") are a list with `mark: cross`; requirements met or promises are `mark: check`.
- A process or chain ("views lead to traffic, traffic to sales"): `flow`, one stage per step.
- A brand, app or company: `logo`. A person, organisation or title: `name`. A place: `map` when the place is the point, else `name`.
- A physical thing or body part said aloud (calculator, phone, brain, heart, tooth, money bag, trophy): `object`, with `visual` naming it in the speaker's words.
- A trend or change (grew, dropped, doubled, falling): `chart` with `trend` up or down.
- Software, search, messages, notifications, a checklist on a phone: `ui` with `visual` one of search, notification, chat, checklist.
- An abstract idea or a story moment (lost, pressure, hope, success, a raid, a meeting): `image`, with `visual` naming the scene in 2-6 plain words from what was said.
- The line that lands the point, short and strong (6 words or fewer): `statement`, a full-screen card.
- A word that carries the sentence: `keyword`, 1-3 words exactly as said, often `behind` the speaker; `label` may hold the 1-3 words said just before it as the small lead.
- "Comment X", "DM me", "follow", "link in bio": `cta`, the word in capitals.

Pacing and taste:
- One beat every 2-3 seconds of the clip; never two at once; nothing in the first half second.
- Mix the kinds. Several `keyword` beats in a row read as plain captions; prefer the specific kind.
- Cards (`statement`, `image`, `place: card`) at most one per five seconds; they replace the speaker, so use them on the line that matters most.
- Every text is the speaker's own words (number words written as digits). Never invent, round or translate a figure or a name.
- Match the tone: a serious health or money point gets clean, calm beats; hype gets bold ones; never a joke on a sad line.
- Pick the clip's `theme`: `clean` (white cards, bright accent) for most advice and business; `dark` (glowing on near-black) for tech, money, trading and hype; `editorial` (cream paper, serif, red and black) for stories, history, documentary and thoughtful podcasts; `bold` (one saturated brand colour) for ads, offers and local businesses.
