# Before/After reels: what the editors actually do

84 reels studied (frames every ~1 s + word-timed transcript + cut/beat data). Per-reel notes are in Part 2.
Part 1 is the pattern across all of them: what turns a raw talking head into the "After".

---

## Part 1. The patterns

### 1. The one rule: every spoken idea gets a picture, on the word
The After is never "captions + music". In nearly every reel, when the speaker **names something**, it appears
on screen at that word:

| What is said | What appears (seen in) |
|---|---|
| A **number** ("27,000", "90%", "1886", "24 years", "$11,725") | Huge number, often counting up, glowing or behind the subject (DRZkc5, DY9i5, Dd6zU, DZHm2, Da7Li) |
| A **list** ("no plan, no direction, no results"; "formula, product, marketing") | Chips / pills / icon tiles that **add one per item** in sync, then stay as a stack (DR932, DXxpK, DYNBK, DdD7V, Dcs-5, DdtLt) |
| A **negation list** ("not rigged, not spreads, not news") | Red ✗ tiles accumulate (DYNBK, DdtLt, Dcx3z) |
| A **checklist / qualifications** | ✓ ticks or badges as said (DYNBK, DcRLe "Board certified ✓") |
| A **brand / app / company** (Swiggy, ChatGPT, Claude, RE/MAX, TCS, Coca-Cola) | Its logo pops beside the head or a card with the logo (DRZkc5, DTaQm, DWnke, DdD_q, DY9i5) |
| A **person** (Zig Ziglar, Iman Gadzhi, Mark Tilbury young) | Photo cut-out with name tag (Dd_nk, DbBnx, DKC2A) |
| A **place** (Gurugram, Bengal, Bihar, Orlando, Kolkata) | Map that draws on / zooms in, or place name as a giant title behind the subject (DWl0q, DTrla, DTw5k, DcEpj) |
| A **product / process** (Whisper, Parakeet, ChatGPT search, Google reviews) | Recreated **UI mock-up** with a cursor clicking (DYe_v, DdD7V, DaqgsL, DeCFx) |
| A **body part / medical thing** (thyroid, kidney, heart, brain, collagen) | 3D model or anatomy illustration (DR932, Dbijq, Dc3Ve, DUDpO, Dd_VN) |
| A **trend / change** ("views → traffic → sales", "drop in hormones", "portfolio grew") | Animated chart: line, bar or flow diagram (DV_xq, DcaP2, DeAQh, Dcx3z) |
| An **abstract idea** ("lost", "pressure", "immediate reward", "where you'll be in 5 years") | A realistic metaphor image (man on rubble peak, trophy above a hand, man on a glowing pillar) (DbL2b, DcXhr) |
| A **story / news event** (documentary) | One realistic, often AI-generated cinematic still per sentence with slow push-in + small labels (DTrla, DTw5k) |
| A **feeling word** in a podcast (crisis, despair, love) | Matching emotional B-roll / film still (DZwlp, DbYJy) |

Rough count: in a 15–30 s After there are **5–12 such graphic beats**, i.e. one every 2–3 s.
The speaker is on screen only 40–75 % of the time; in documentaries and explainers 25–30 %.

### 2. The layer stack (what every After has)
1. **Grade**: raw is flat/grey; After is warm teal-orange, or a brand palette. Many "Before" panels are shown in B&W on purpose.
2. **Reframe rhythm**: alternate wide ↔ tight crop every sentence (punch-in on the key line). Even one-take reels with **zero cuts** (DTK_x, DXwoT) feel edited purely from punch-ins.
3. **Tightened speech**: pauses and false starts gone; cuts land on beats (on-beat ratio 0.75–1.0 is common).
4. **2-tier captions**: small light lead words + one BIG keyword per line (bold sans or script/serif italic), keyword in the accent colour, often with glow. Captions sit centre-low, in the same eye zone as the face (DcobI makes this its whole lesson).
5. **Text behind subject**: the key word or a giant number set behind the rotoscoped speaker (~35 % of reels).
6. **Graphic beats** (table above), full-screen cards or overlays beside the head.
7. **Transitions between beats**: white/colour flash, light leak, whip/slide, circle wipe, pixel dissolve, rack-focus blur, glitch.
8. **SFX**: whoosh on every card in, pop/click on each chip, ding on ticks, riser into the hook (the measured "hits" run every 0.1–0.3 s in most After audio).
9. **Brand + CTA**: logo tracked in the scene, phone number pill, "DM me", "Comment X", Instagram profile mock.

### 3. The card styles that keep coming back
- **Editorial paper**: cream/red/black, serif titles, halftone B&W cut-outs, paper texture (DR932, DXpQr, DdblM, DdhBP, DS1V8).
- **Apple-minimal**: white/grey card, one icon or 3D object, small black text, lots of air (DUDpO, DZHm2, DcGw1, DdAEK).
- **Dark neon**: black/navy/purple with glowing keywords, neon frames, glass pills (DaFaI, Dcs-5, Dcx3z, DdtLt).
- **Brand-colour solid**: one full brand colour card (pink medical, green landscaping, blue agency) with a stat or list (DcaP2, DaqgsL, Dbo4j).
- **Collage/grid**: photo tiles that stack or mosaic for lists of examples (DcLlg, DdEI6, DeCFx).
- **Realistic cinematic / AI stills**: documentaries, health and "hope" moments (DTrla, DTw5k, DcRLe, DdEI6).

### 4. Formats (what the raw is, and the recipe that fits)
| Raw type | Count | Recipe |
|---|---|---|
| Creator / coach talking head (advice, money, growth) | ~30 | 2-tier captions + number/list/logo beats + 1–2 metaphor images + CTA |
| Local business / service ad (dentist, realtor, car wash, landscaper, salon, breeder, agency) | ~25 | Pain → proof (reviews, credentials, logos, years) → offer (price, timer, % off) → CTA with phone/handle. Brand palette everywhere. |
| Podcast clip (Raj Shamani, MrBeast, Jay Shetty, Manoj Bajpayee, Paresh Rawal) | ~15 | Multi-cam cuts, keyword captions, cut-out speaker on solid colour cards, film B-roll for feelings, lists as chips |
| Documentary / news narration (Hindi political, history) | 4 | Speaker ~25 %; one realistic scene per sentence, maps that zoom, name labels, newspaper clippings |
| Pure motion-design explainer (voice only or brain/dopamine) | 3 | Graphics replace footage entirely: timelines, node diagrams, halftone collages |
| Product / prop demo (collagen balloon, toothpaste, Parakeet app) | ~5 | Product shots, UI recreations with cursor, macro B-roll |

Languages: ~55 % English, ~30 % Hindi/Hinglish, plus Telugu, Persian, Arabic, Portuguese, Serbian. The graphics carry meaning across languages, and captions are often bilingual.

### 6. What this means for Clipnera (gaps against today's engine)
1. **A "graphic beat" planner**: from the transcript, find numbers, lists, negations, brands, people, places, products, trends and abstract ideas, and give each one a beat type + time span. That's the GPT planning step the user asked for.
2. **Templates per beat type** (count-up number, list chips that add one per item, ✗/✓ stacks, logo pop, name card, map zoom, UI mock with cursor, line/bar chart, text-behind-subject). Each one gets an SFX pairing.
3. **Image sourcing per beat**: logos, person photos, 3D icons, realistic metaphor or documentary stills (AI-generated where licensing allows), matching emotional B-roll.
4. **Subject cut-out** (rotoscope) for text-behind and solid-colour cards: used in about a third of the Afters.
5. **Reframe rhythm + grade presets** (warm teal-orange, brand palette, B&W for the "before").
6. **Brand/CTA layer** from the brand kit (logo tracking, phone pill, handle, profile mock).
7. **Benchmark set**: the 6 shared test clips above.
