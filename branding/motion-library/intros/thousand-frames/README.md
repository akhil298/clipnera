| | |

## What happens

Hundreds of tiny video frames swirl like a galaxy, gather into the sparkle shape, turn amber, collapse into the tile and burst out as glitter. The letters fade in as their spacing tightens. Tagline: "Hours of footage. One spark."

## Why it fits Clipnera

All of a creator's footage, and the AI's one spark that picks what matters.

## Re-render

```sh
node branding/motion/source/render.mjs library --only thousand-frames
```
