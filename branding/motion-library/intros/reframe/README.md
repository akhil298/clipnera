## What happens

A wide video with a face-tracking box on the speaker. A 9:16 crop window follows them, then shrinks into the tile, and the name pops in word by word like Clipnera's karaoke captions. Tagline: "Landscape in. Shorts out."

## Why it fits Clipnera

Shows reframing to vertical, the other half of the product. The crop window literally becomes the logo.

## Re-render

```sh
node branding/motion/source/render.mjs library --only reframe
```
